Delete your Rockso account

Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Purpose

This page explains how to request deletion of your Rockso account and its associated data. The process is accessible without reinstalling the app.

2. How to request deletion

In the app, open Settings → Account → deletion. On the Web, sign in to Rockso deletion management using your usual credentials; this account-management route requires no Coach account and remains accessible after uninstalling the app. You may also email support@rockso.app.

Choose your Athlete workspace, Coach workspace or entire account, review the scope, then confirm your identity and request. Preparing a request does not start deletion. You receive a reference and can view its progress.

3. Data deleted

Deletion covers personal data belonging to the selected workspace: sports profile, activities and physiological measurements, personal sessions and plans, conversations and related analyses, imported files, relevant connections and tokens. The relevant synchronisations stop to prevent data from being recreated.

You may delete only your Athlete or Coach workspace and keep the other. Entire-account deletion covers both workspaces and login credentials. Data belonging to other users is outside your request.

After Athlete deletion, their Coach loses access to their individual data and their contribution to statistics. The Coach keeps their own creations with verified provenance and generic versions of verified prescriptions, without the deleted Athlete’s identity, results, health data, personal notes or links. No individual history or cohort contribution from that Athlete is retained for this purpose.

4. Data that may be retained temporarily

Minimal technical evidence of processing (references, dates and digests, without activity or health content) is retained for at most 90 days from the initial processing deadline shown on your confirmation. Some evidence may expire earlier. Retrying processing does not extend this limit.

The address used for the completion email is erased from the delivery register once the provider accepts the message or delivery is abandoned. Provider acceptance does not guarantee inbox delivery.

Residual backup or security copies have separate purposes and expiry cycles, described in the privacy policy. Records strictly necessary for a legal obligation or dispute are limited to the applicable duration and are not used to reactivate your account. Necessary requests to processors are tracked; they do not delete your account with the original sports platform.

5. Processing timelines

At confirmation, choose either 25 days in which you can cancel or processing without a cancellation delay. The selected delay starts at confirmation; your receipt shows the exact deadline. You may cancel before that time. Irreversible erasure cannot begin before it; once it starts, reversal is no longer guaranteed.

Processing is asynchronous. Status distinguishes a confirmed request, ongoing operations and genuinely verified completion. We confirm completion to you. The 25-day period is your chosen waiting period, not a promise of instantaneous erasure when it ends.

We acknowledge support requests within seven calendar days at most. We respond within applicable legal deadlines, normally within one month of receipt; any legally justified extension and its reasons are communicated within that period.

6. Consequences

Once processing is complete, erased data is no longer accessible and its deletion is permanent. A retained workspace remains usable; entire-account deletion ends access to the service.

Deleting Rockso data does not delete the original data or account held by your sports platforms. Refusing or withdrawing permission to send data to external AI is a separate action: it stops relevant new transfers without automatically deleting your Rockso data.

7. Contact

Questions about this process: support@rockso.app. Other data rights or requests concerning specific data: privacy@rockso.app. See the privacy policy.
