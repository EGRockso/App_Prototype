Privacy policy

Last updated : 18 October 2026

1. Controller and scope

AIMS Corp., a French SAS registered under number 941 923 682 RCS Paris, is the controller for the processing described here. Its full identity and postal address appear in the legal notice. For personal data: privacy@rockso.app.

This policy covers rockso.app, its demo and beta access, and the free Rockso app. The Service may be used by adults and minors under the conditions explained below and in the Terms. Rockso supports sports training; its indicators and recommendations are neither a diagnosis nor medical care.

The President of AIMS Corp. is responsible for personal information protection. You can contact this person at privacy@rockso.app or at the AIMS Corp. postal address given in the legal notice.

2. Data and sources

The data processed depends on the features used and the sources you connect.

  • Account and access: email address, name or identifier, authentication data, preferences, language, account creation and login dates. Supabase manages authentication.
  • Sports profile: sports, goals, availability, level and information you provide, such as birth date, sex, height or weight.
  • Training and wellbeing: planned and completed sessions, duration, distance, elevation, power, cadence, heart rate, load, recovery, sleep, fatigue, perceived effort and any pain or injury information you provide; some indicators are calculated from this data.
  • Location: where an authorised import contains a route, GPS coordinates and activity timestamps. Source permissions and settings determine access to the route.
  • AI exchanges: messages, sports context used to answer, responses, reports and conversation summaries retained for continuity of support.
  • Connections: connection identifiers, permissions and tokens required to synchronise with sports platforms and, where available, Apple Health / HealthKit or Health Connect. Rockso does not ask for these platforms’ passwords.
  • Support, waitlist and early access: emails, requests, feedback and information submitted through forms; optional role and organisation, language and form source for the waitlist.
  • Diagnostics and security: IP address, device information, operating system, app version, technical events, error and login logs. Sentry may receive the user identifier and email address associated with an event, together with its technical context.

Sources include your entries, use of the Service, authorised platforms and Rockso’s calculations. Providing information about other people in messages or notes is unnecessary for the Service.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Create an account, manage access, display and synchronise training, and provide requested features: performance of the contract or steps taken at your request before entering a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), for necessary data. Without essential account information, these features cannot be provided.
  • Calculate indicators and personalise support: performance of the contract for necessary sports data that is not health data; explicit consent for health data and its associated uses (Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a)). Optional data enriches chosen features and is not a general condition for visiting the website.
  • Answer support requests, manage the waitlist and requested launch information, and organise requested beta access: performance of the contract or pre-contractual steps; legitimate interest in handling other requests addressed to Rockso (Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Detect and fix errors, secure the Service and prevent abuse: legitimate interest in a reliable service and account protection (Article 6(1)(f)), limited to necessary data. This basis alone does not authorise processing health data.
  • Handle rights, legal obligations and disputes: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)) and legitimate interest in defending rights for strictly necessary records (Article 6(1)(f)). Retaining health data for legal claims is limited to the conditions of Article 9(2)(f).

A new optional use requiring consent is subject to separate information and a separate choice. Accepting the Terms does not mean consenting to every processing operation.

4. Health data, permissions and withdrawal

Information about pain, injuries or physiological condition, and certain combinations or inferences from sports data, may constitute health data. Collection, analysis and transmission for the chosen features require your prior explicit consent.

A technical permission given to Apple Health, Health Connect, a watch or another platform does not by itself replace this consent. Permissions are limited to the categories required for the requested feature; you can change them in the source’s settings and disconnect the source.

You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting privacy@rockso.app, and use the available settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. Health permission is required to use your Athlete workspace. If you refuse or withdraw it, access to Athlete features and their dependent processing stops. Account management and the exercise of your rights remain accessible. This choice does not automatically delete Rockso data or close the account. You can make a separate erasure request; your rights and legal retention limits remain applicable. Disconnecting a source stops new imports but does not automatically delete previously imported data: you can request its erasure.

Rockso does not use health data, including data from HealthKit or Health Connect, for advertising, selling data or training models.

Minors and parental consent

Available routes depend on the country or territory, declared age and qualified conditions. Declaring birth date and residence is not identity verification. The parental-authorisation route is deferred: cases requiring that authorisation remain unavailable. The free individual Athlete route for French residents aged 15–17, where offered, supports Rockso use without external AI transfer; features requiring that transfer and the minor Coach route remain unavailable. Connected platforms’ own restrictions also apply. No worldwide availability or universal legal age of 18 is claimed.

Minors are not barred from access solely because they are under 18. Required permission or representation for use of the Service depends on age, legal capacity and applicable law. It does not replace consent required for personal data processing.

In France, for processing based on consent in an online service, before age 15, joint consent from the minor and the holder of parental responsibility is required. From age 15, the minor may consent independently to such processing. Ages and conditions may differ in other countries. These rules also apply to explicit consent required for health data; age does not remove the need to obtain it.

The minor may exercise their rights. Their legal representative may contact privacy@rockso.app regarding the account or data, including a deletion request in the child’s best interests. We assess their standing and request proportionately, respecting the minor’s rights.

5. Artificial intelligence and conversations

Where available in your access route, some features use the Mistral AI API to generate responses, activity analyses, summaries and training adaptations. These features send messages and relevant sports context to Mistral: training history or summaries, indicators, perceived effort, recovery information and conversation context. This may include health data you provided or authorised.

External AI transfer is optional. You can use Rockso without it. Refusing or withdrawing that permission in Account stops new transfers relying on it without automatically deleting data already held in Rockso. Internal calculations and models running without that transfer are separate processing; health processing remains subject to its own permission. Withdrawal cannot physically recall a transfer already made: exercising erasure rights with recipients is a separate process.

Transmission of personal data to this provider requires prior information and explicit permission for the relevant features; health data requirements apply in addition. This is not a model running solely on your device.

Rockso does not reuse your personal data or conversations to train models. The provider’s technical processing is governed by its commercial terms and data processing agreement. The Mistral configuration selected for Rockso excludes use of conversations to train its models. Generating a response and training a model are distinct operations.

Summaries and responses may be inaccurate; you can check them and request correction of personal data. Rockso does not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you based solely on automated processing. Its sports recommendations remain suggestions.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Account and training data: retention is limited to the period necessary for a validly applicable purpose, taking your choices and rights into account. Using Athlete history and features remains subject to health permission; refusing or withdrawing it stops that access and dependent processing without automatically triggering erasure. Stopping use of the app does not delete the account.
  • Support, waitlist or early access requests: retained while handling the request and its necessary follow-up; only records required for a legal obligation or evidence of a right are then retained for the applicable limitation period.
  • Requested deletion: choose the entire account or one Athlete/Coach workspace, then confirm your identity and an optional 25-day cancellation period, or processing without that delay. Data in the other workspace and other users’ data are preserved. Status confirms genuinely verified completion; see the process and timelines. The Coach keeps only their own creations and generic prescriptions with verified provenance, without the deleted Athlete’s individual data or statistical contribution.
  • Minimal technical evidence: references, dates and digests without activity or health content, retained for at most 90 days from the initial processing deadline shown on the confirmation, without extensions on retry; some evidence expires earlier. The completion-email address is removed from the delivery register once the provider accepts the message or delivery is abandoned. This period is distinct from backup retention.
  • Residual technical backups and security logs: expire, are deleted or overwritten within 90 days after deletion, without reuse for training support. If technical restoration is required during this period, deletion requests must be reapplied. This rule concerns the account and training-support data described above; retention and erasure of public-website analytics events are governed by the dedicated supplement.
  • Legal exceptions: strictly necessary records may be retained for a legal obligation or dispute, with restricted access, for the legally required period or the duration of proceedings and applicable limitation period. This does not justify retaining the entire sports profile.

The account deletion page explains the process accessible without reinstalling the app. Deactivation or loss of access alone is not data deletion.

9. Security

We protect data through encrypted communications, authentication and access restrictions. Technical logs help detect and correct incidents. No system provides absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs, we apply notification requirements for authorities and, where the risk requires it, affected people.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR’s conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability of your data. You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation, and withdraw consent where used. Under French law, you may also give instructions about your data after your death.

Contact privacy@rockso.app or the postal address in the legal notice. Additional identity evidence is requested only when there is reasonable doubt, proportionately. For requests governed by the EU or UK GDPR, we respond without undue delay and within one month of receiving a request. A two-month extension may be necessary under that regime’s conditions due to complexity or the number of requests; you are informed of the extension and reasons within the first month.

For access and correction requests governed by Canadian law, we respond within 30 days; we apply an extension only where the relevant law permits it and subject to its conditions. In Switzerland, information requested under the right of access is normally provided within 30 days. In Australia, we respond within a reasonable period, generally within 30 days. We explain the reasons for any refusal and the available remedies. Where several laws apply, their rights and remedies apply together; we respect the shortest applicable deadline. An extension permitted by one regime does not extend a deadline that another applicable regime does not allow us to extend.

To complain about how your information is handled, email privacy@rockso.app and describe the problem. The person responsible for personal information protection reviews the complaint, may ask for necessary details, and provides a response explaining the action taken. We aim to respond within 30 days. This process does not limit your rights to seek a remedy or applicable legal deadlines.

You may contact the competent authority: the CNIL or your country’s data protection authority in the EEA; the ICO in the United Kingdom; the FDPIC in Switzerland; the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the relevant provincial authority, including the Commission d’accès à l’information in Québec. You may exercise your right to complain under the EU or UK GDPR without first contacting us.

For the Australian complaint procedure, contact us first; if the response does not resolve the problem, or we do not respond within a reasonable period, generally 30 days, you may complain to the OAIC. This preliminary step for that procedure does not limit a separate remedy available to you under the GDPR.

Joining the launch waitlist

The operator’s email provider receives that message. You do not need to join Discord to sign up; these notifications are not sent to PostHog and are not shared for advertising.

Optional product analytics — mobile and Coach

AIMS Corp. produces internal registration and confirmed-action statistics from the records needed to operate Rockso, including accounts, completed onboarding, waitlist signups and Coach actions. These business statistics are independent of PostHog acceptance. They are distinct from page or screen counts, sessions and unique users.

Detailed behavioural analytics in the mobile app and Coach require a separate, optional prior agreement for each surface. With your agreement, Supabase and PostHog EU receive predefined screen and feature names, interactions, journeys, observed sessions and estimated active time, together with the platform and app version. Account identifiers remain internal to Supabase; PostHog receives a random Analytics pseudonym stable by account and surface for consented retention measurement and the handling of rights requests and temporary session or journey identifiers. No email, conversation content, sports or health measurement, activity identifier or full URL is included. Advertising profiling, autocapture, heatmaps and Session Replay are disabled. The website choice does not authorise this product analytics.

You can accept or refuse with equally accessible controls, and withdraw in mobile Settings or Coach Account without affecting the Service. The choice is valid for 180 days and stored by account and surface. A local refusal also remains effective for 180 days if the server is unavailable. The local copy may remain until replaced or until app or browser storage is cleared; the server record and pseudonym mapping are retained under the conditions below. The targets are 90 days for new detailed Supabase events and 397 days (about 13 months) for account/day presence and feature records, to measure returns up to one year. Fine-grained working counters are retained for at most 12 months. De-identified monthly results awaiting qualification remain protected working caches, with a target retention of 15 months and rebuilding after the relevant contributions are erased. Only results whose effective anonymity has been verified may then be retained while useful, with an annual review. Reducing dimensions, rounding and protecting small groups are precautions; aggregation alone does not establish anonymity. First business milestones remain associated with the account to prevent duplicate conversions. Individual Supabase records are removed for the relevant space when it is erased, or for all spaces on full account erasure. Effectively anonymous historical statistics remain; they do not count an erased account among currently active users. Pseudonyms needed for an erasure request are retained until its processing by the provider has been verified; erasure of PostHog events requires a tracked provider request, without a promise of immediate or automatic deletion; withdrawal alone does not erase past events. The PostHog provider, transfers, retention limitations and rights described below also apply to detailed product analytics. Additional interface measurement before agreement is not enabled in this version; aggregation alone is not treated as an exemption from consent.

This supplement applies only to the French and English rockso.app home pages and their waitlist forms. It does not change the application's health-data or AI permissions. AIMS Corp. may offer audience measurement with PostHog Cloud EU to measure visits to home and form pages, acquisition sources, confirmed new waitlist signups and clicks on genuine download links, without advertising profiling. Your choice: audience measurement relies on your prior consent. Accepting and refusing are equally simple. Closing the window without accepting is not consent. For an erasure request, contact privacy@rockso.app.

Limited data: event name, time, random temporary visit identifier (pseudonymised data), predefined page and language, general platform (iOS, Android, desktop or unknown), validated UTM campaign parameters and, for downloads, destination platform and button placement. The four events are landing_page_viewed, early_access_page_viewed, early_access_submitted and download_clicked. UTM parameters are reserved for campaign labels and must not contain personal data. A confirmed signup means a successful new Supabase record, without email-address verification or automatic beta admission. Form contents, emails including hashed emails, account identifiers, sports or health data, full URLs, fragments, referrers and advertising identifiers are not sent to PostHog. Autocapture, heatmaps, Session Replay and unnecessary automatic collection are disabled. OAuth pages, beta invitations and demos are not instrumented.

After your agreement to website audience measurement, copies without a visit or account identifier feed aggregate page, source, confirmed-signup signal and genuine-download-click counters in Supabase, with a 12-month retention target. The reference count of new waitlist signups comes from confirmed Supabase records and does not depend on this choice.

Storage: your choice, its date and the information version are recorded in this browser’s local storage. The choice remains valid for 180 days, whether you accept or refuse. After expiry, it no longer authorises collection and you are asked again on an eligible page. The local record may remain until replaced or cleared in your browser. After consent, a random identifier and validated UTMs are kept in tab storage, with logical expiry after 30 minutes without a measured event; they are removed upon withdrawal and, normally, when the tab closes. The SDK keeps Analytics data in temporary memory and a technical consent indicator in local storage; that indicator is removed upon withdrawal and cannot authorise collection without a still-valid choice. No identifier is shared with Coach or the mobile application. Clearing local storage may cause the consent choice to appear again.

Provider and network: PostHog Inc. acts as AIMS Corp.’s processor under a data processing agreement signed by both companies. The project is hosted in the European Union, primarily in Frankfurt. Transit, support or subprocessing operations may nevertheless involve processing or access outside the European Economic Area, governed by the DPA and applicable safeguards. Network requests necessarily transmit an IP address and technical metadata to the provider. “Discard client IP data” is enabled to prevent IP storage in events; the SDK disables geographic enrichment for transmitted events. Choosing the EU region alone does not guarantee that all operations remain within the European Union. See the PostHog processing agreement and its subprocessors.

Event retention — activation pending: AIMS Corp. has selected a target retention period of 12 months from collection to measure the results of Rockso’s website and launch. PostHog Free currently provides a one-year event query window; this query limit does not guarantee automatic deletion at that point. The process for erasing events without person profiles, including events no longer queryable, actual deletion timelines and backup handling still need confirmation from PostHog before activation. This target is therefore not yet an operational guarantee of deletion within 12 months. The rights and contacts in section 10 continue to apply.

Website events are not linked to your email address or Rockso account. To handle an access or erasure request, we may need information allowing us to locate the relevant events. Deletion may require asynchronous processing by PostHog; requests are followed up within applicable legal deadlines, without promising immediate erasure. Deleting a Rockso account does not, by itself, allow us to locate and erase website visits.
